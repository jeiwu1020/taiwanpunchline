# V8: editorial caricature style correction

Only the Su Tseng-chang and Chen Shui-bian portrait textures are replaced. The user preferred V7 likeness but requested the satirical comic style of the other characters. V8 uses the V7 identities and the official photo references below, with Wang/Han/Ma as style-only references.

Su has a larger bald dome, raised cheeks, crescent-shaped smiling eyes, asymmetric grin, and tapered jaw. Chen keeps the orderly side part, gold glasses, horizontal eyes and closed mouth, with broader cheeks and a smaller chin. Both turn slightly right in three-quarter view and have stronger contours and simpler shading. Chen's arched eyebrow and smirk are a deliberate caricature; perceived likeness remains subjective.

Sources used only as identity references, not distributed game assets:
- Su: Executive Yuan historical portrait, https://history.ey.gov.tw/director/?director=34
- Chen: Presidential Office former president portrait, https://www.president.gov.tw/Page/87 (image credit: 國史館).

Image generation produced transparent 1254×1254 PNGs. WebP conversion is lossless and preserves RGBA pixel values exactly, with no programmatic alteration of the artwork. Both originals remain available in prior versioned assets. The generated images contain faint edge alpha and mostly near-opaque skin; browser integration checks determine whether this produces any visible seam. Short neck attachments are part of the generated heads.

No body, hand, prop, pose, joint, hitbox, move, control, sound or voice asset changes. The existing private audience is preserved. These references do not establish public or commercial reuse rights, and no voice rights have been newly cleared.
