# V6 final roster assets

Added original generated Chen Shui-bian and Ko Wen-je transparent head portraits, a two-row costume atlas, and an integrated pair of hand/forearm/garbage-bag cutouts. Chen wears charcoal green with a pale green tie. Ko wears an off-white casual jacket with charcoal trousers. These use the same editorial ink and crosshatch style as V5.

Built-in image generation used the V5 Ma/Huang portraits as style references for heads, and the V5 costume atlas for two new rows. Prompts requested recognizable mature public figures facing screen right; complete head silhouettes; warm peach skin; no text, body or neck clothing. Costume prompt requested torso, upper sleeve, right dorsal fist/forearm, left palmar fist/forearm, thigh, shin/shoe in each row. Bag prompt explicitly requested a single connected right dorsal forearm/hand gripping a blue bag and a single connected left palmar forearm/hand gripping a green bag, not floating props. Five-digit identity and skin tone were explicit constraints. Assets are generated satire, not photographs or endorsements.

Final project files: assets/chen-head-v6.webp, ko-head-v6.webp, final-costumes-v6.webp and ko-bags-v6.webp. Heads are 1254×1254; atlases are 1536×1024. PNG-to-WebP conversion was lossless with exact hidden RGB preservation and full RGBA byte equality. No script repainted or edited image content. These four images add about 6.15 MiB compressed and 24.0 MiB decoded pixels. All roster images currently preload, so older phones may have a noticeable startup or memory cost.

Far left arm and green bag draw behind the torso; near right arm and blue bag draw in front. Bag and hand are a single sprite so grip remains attached during rotation. Body movement still uses fixed arm segments and solved elbows, and whole-character mirroring remains a deliberate first-version limitation. Bags appear with the move and disappear on recovery or interruption; there is no pickup/stow animation. New characters have one principal facial expression each.

No new real speech or voice cloning was added. Five new characters use synthesized effect sounds. The prior Han original excerpt is unchanged.
